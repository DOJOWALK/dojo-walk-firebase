# DOJO WALK Brand Guidelines

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved working palette

## Brand colour hierarchy

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Vibrant Orange | #F28C28 | Primary actions, calls to action, brand highlights and logo accent |
| Secondary | Royal Blue | #174A78 | Navigation, headings, trust and supporting brand elements |
| Supporting | Light Sky Blue | #E8F3FA | Soft panels, cards and secondary surfaces |
| Main surface | Warm White | #FFFDF9 | Main app and website backgrounds |
| Text | Dark Charcoal | #263442 | Body text and high-contrast content |

## Usage principles

- Orange is the primary brand colour; blue is the secondary brand colour.
- Use orange for the most important action on a screen, not for every control.
- Use blue for navigation, headings and trust-oriented information.
- Use warm white and light sky blue to keep layouts calm and readable.
- Keep text contrast accessible. Do not place small white text on light orange.
- Status colours (success, warning, error) must remain semantically distinct from brand colours.
- Do not hard-code unrelated colour values across apps; reuse these tokens where practical.

## Product consistency

Apply this hierarchy to the website, customer app, walker app, admin panel, digital assets and future marketing materials. The logo may use orange for its symbol and blue for its wordmark, subject to final logo approval.

## Brand assets

The palette is approved as the working brand system. The final logo artwork, icon exports, typography files and trademark clearance are separate deliverables.
